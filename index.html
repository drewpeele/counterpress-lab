Phone in your pocket, one thumb. Tap a drill to get the interval timer, the constraint, and your cues on one screen. It beeps the changeovers and keeps your screen awake. Build a session first and it shows up here in order.

The drills only work if your reaction matches them. Every constraint in the library asks a kid to take a risk. If your voice punishes the risk when it fails, the rule stops mattering by week three. These are the words, ready before you need them.

Principles adapted from Jürgen Klopp and Pep Lijnders. Physical work is age-scaled for U11: short doses at full intensity, everything with a ball, acceleration and braking over endurance running. No laps in here on purpose.
